You don't need to have it figured out.

Most of us came the first time unsure, tired, or just curious. Harbor & Light started twelve years ago as a handful of families meeting in a living room, and it's still shaped by that size — even now that the room is bigger. We read scripture together, ask hard questions out loud, and try to build a life around what we find.

Whatever brought you here today, there's a seat for you Sunday. No forms to fill out beforehand, no expectation you'll come back — just an invitation.
